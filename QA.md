# Release verification

Verified locally on 25-27 October and 18-19 November 2026 against the production build, not just the development server. This record covers the illustrated rewrite, the subsequent onboarding/settings polish, the guided-v2 depth rewrite of all 544 lessons, the November reading-comfort pass, and the lesson pictures and connected ideas added on 18 and 19 November. The previous condensed release passed 280 unit checks and 105 browser cases; those are not used as evidence for this revision.

## Results

### Lesson pictures and connected ideas, 18 and 19 November

The owner asked for more images in every lesson from any source, including image searches and meme images, with attribution that does not get in the way, for more context where a lesson was thin, and for links between lessons that explain each other. What was built is narrower, and the difference is deliberate. This repository is public, so a commit publishes each file. Pictures therefore come only from Wikimedia Commons, under public domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA terms. No image-search results and no meme templates were used. Widening the sources is the owner's decision. The rules that were followed are in the Pictures by other people and Connected lessons sections of [docs/TEACHING.md](docs/TEACHING.md), which the owner has not reviewed.

- Coverage: 133 pictures in 124 of the 544 lessons, 5.68 MiB in total. Cinema has 38 files in 37 lessons, design 31 in 27, field guides 10 in 9, life 13, thinking 11 in 10, money 8, tech 8 in 7, observer 4, mind 4 in 3, work 3 and wellbeing 3. No picture was searched for in the people, speaker, presence and perspective topics, whose lessons are about conversations and judgments. A lesson gets a picture only where an openly licensed file shows the real effect, tool, place, artwork or case the lesson is about. A picture in every lesson was not attempted.
- Five of the pictures are memory hooks, in design-018, design-030, observer-004, observer-021 and money-009: an openly licensed picture with one line of UNSCROLL text under it.
- Lesson bodies were not rewritten. The added context is in the captions, each of which ties its picture to the lesson's own example, and in the reasons shown with connected lessons.
- Connected ideas: 108 pairs between 202 lessons. 86 pairs join lessons from different categories, and no lesson has more than three. The 80 pairs added on 19 November were each written after reading the stated outcome of both lessons. No person has reviewed the pairs or their reasons.

### Guided-v2 depth rewrite, 26-27 October

The owner reviewed the illustrated release and found the lessons still too compressed to learn from: every body sat in a 230-303 word band, several new terms arrived per paragraph, and scope hedges interrupted explanations. The guided-v2 contract in docs/TEACHING.md replaced that edition. Every one of the 544 lessons was rewritten by hand, module by module, with a pushed checkpoint per module, in hardest-first order: tech, perspective, work, thinking, mind, field guides, presence, wellbeing, speaker, observer, life, money, people, the two design modules, and the three cinema modules.

Each lesson now follows one arc in four sections: a concrete situation the reader recognizes and why it matters; an everyday analogy that introduces the mechanism and then names its term; a walked example with the original numbers and timings restated; and the boundaries and common misuses, closing with two habits. The whole lesson changed register, not only the body: captions say what to look at first, practice steps carry their reason, notice and troubleshooting text describe the observable result and its absence, and recall answers and quiz explanations reuse the lesson's analogy. Lesson IDs, quiz correct answers, visual specifications (types, items, arrows, colours, source notes), and source links are unchanged, so no local notes, completions, or collections are affected and no diagram is re-downloaded.

Measured over the whole library with scripts/analyze-teaching-depth.ts after the final module: body words minimum 420, median 649, maximum 838; Flesch-Kincaid grade median 7.3, maximum 10.4; average sentence length median 14.6 words, 90th percentile 16.1. No lesson is below 420 words or above the 10.5 grade ceiling. The 147 remaining hedge phrases sit in boundaries sections and honesty labels (invented, hypothetical, illustrative), where the contract allows them. The content validator applies the 420-900 word band, the grade ceiling and the 17-word sentence limit per lesson; the unit contract asserts every lesson is tagged guided-v2 with exactly four distinct sections inside that band.

## Environments and limitations

Playwright emulated mobile Chromium and WebKit at 390 x 844 and desktop Chromium at 1280 x 800. Responsive cases also resized each engine to the five widths above. Browser tests use deterministic discovery responses; a separate smoke check exercises Wikimedia.

Passing tests do not prove every device or future sequence is fault-free. No physical phone was available to verify operating-system installation, virtual keyboards, battery use, screen readers, or storage eviction. These need a final check on the owner's phone after HTTPS deployment.

The dark theme has been seen only in emulated browsers, and only eight captures of the final build were inspected by eye. It has not been checked on an OLED phone in a dark room, where near-black surfaces and the dimmed diagrams matter most. The pull-to-refresh rule applies only to an installed app; browser automation cannot emulate that display mode, so the test reads the shipped CSS rule and does not exercise the gesture. None of the four install routes in docs/phone-install-options.md was run on a phone.

The lesson pictures have been seen only by the assistant that added them: on contact sheets, and in emulated phone captures.
